Ask Insights: Inclusive Leadership Journey

Inclusive Leadership Journey

The Inclusive Leadership Journey is a 14-week leadership development program by Ask Insights, built to help organizational leaders develop the specific skills, empathy, vulnerability, humility, and the ability to create psychological safety, that make inclusion something leaders practice, not just endorse. It's designed as a sustained journey rather than a one-time workshop, because inclusive leadership behavior takes repeated practice to become habit.

Why Inclusive Leadership Has to Come First in DE&I

Most DE&I efforts stall at the same point: policies exist, but the leaders responsible for putting them into practice haven't been equipped to do so. As DE&I awareness grows across organizations, the realization that keeps surfacing is that inclusive leadership competency is the actual starting point, not an add-on once other initiatives are in place.

The gap this closes is a real one. Research reported by Harvard Business Review has found that a large share of underrepresented employees encounter microaggressions at work, things like being interrupted in meetings or facing biased assumptions, and McKinsey's research on workplace representation has shown women of color holding a small fraction of senior leadership roles compared to entry-level ones. Numbers like these are usually a leadership gap before they're a policy gap.

Culture shifts once leaders are genuinely equipped to lead it, particularly across three areas:

  • Representation of diverse groups at every level of the organization, not just entry roles

  • Mindset shifts that move leaders toward practicing inclusion, not just stating intent

  • Policy changes that create equitable opportunities for everyone, backed by leaders who actually apply them

A 14-Week Journey, Not a Workshop

In today's VUCA (volatile, uncertain, complex, ambiguous) environment, leaders need a specific set of skills: empathy, vulnerability, humility, and the ability to create psychological safety for the people they lead. Just as important, they need to be equipped to act as allies, not simply hold the title of one.

That kind of behavior change doesn't come from a single session. It takes repetition, feedback and real workplace application, which is why the Inclusive Leadership Journey runs over 14 weeks rather than compressing into a workshop format. The program is customized for each organization and is built around Ask Insights' RISE Framework [full form and breakdown to be added once confirmed], combining structured learning with practical application so leaders leave with both the capability and the language to become genuine champions and advocates for inclusion.

Who the Inclusive Leadership Journey Is For

This journey is built primarily for people leaders, senior management teams, and high-potential leaders being prepared for greater organizational responsibility. It works well for organizations at any stage of their DE&I journey, whether leadership is building foundational awareness or refining more advanced allyship and inclusive decision-making skills.

Ask Insights' broader work across Gender Intelligence, LGBTQIA+ inclusion, Disability Positive programs, and Developmental Journeys for Diverse Groups often feeds into this leadership work, since inclusive leadership is what makes those more specific programs land inside an organization instead of staying siloed.
